Shortcut's sub-tasks feature lets teams decompose large stories into smaller assigned, estimated work units that can be picked up and moved in parallel, distinct from lightweight checklists. It integrates with Korey, an AI agent that generates structured sub-tasks from rough descriptions, and supports automations that update parent story status as sub-tasks progress.
