Shortcut introduced a redesigned user interface focused on a calmer, clearer, faster, and more focused experience, rebuilt iteratively across the app header, navigation, and individual features. The team tested changes internally and refined spacing, icons, and color palettes based on customer feedback, with 77% of users opting into the new design during a beta rollout.
